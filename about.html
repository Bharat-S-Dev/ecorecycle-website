About Eco Recycle

Building a cleaner and greener future through responsible E-Waste recycling.

Who We Are

EcoRecycle is an awareness-driven E-Waste management platform focused on promoting safe electronic waste disposal and environmental sustainability.

Every year millions of electronic devices are discarded improperly, releasing harmful toxins into our environment. Our mission is to help people recycle electronics responsibly and connect them with authorized recycling centers.

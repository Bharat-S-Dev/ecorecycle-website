Why Recycle E-Waste?

Understanding the impact of your digital footprint.

Stop Toxins

Electronics contain Lead, Mercury, and Cadmium that leak into our soil and water.

Resource Recovery

90% of materials in a laptop or phone like gold, copper, and glass can be reused.

Save Energy

Recycling saves more energy than mining new raw materials from the earth.
